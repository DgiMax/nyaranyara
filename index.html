Storyline

  • Country Kenya
  • Language English, Kiswahili & Sheng
  • Setting Kilifi, Nairobi, Nyeri, Kisumu
  • Release date TBA

A gifted storyteller leaves Kilifi for Nairobi, carrying the gift that once filled his family’s restaurant. In the city he finds humiliation, unexpected friendship, and two formidable collaborators. Together they build a breakthrough act — and when fame finally offers him a place on his own, he risks losing the friendship, and the voice, that made him worth hearing.

Jimmy, Lory and Jitu create Nyara: an electrifying mix of storytelling, satire, music and rhythm that the country starts repeating back to them. Then the industry comes shopping for one sellable star. Overlooked Jimmy secretly takes the deal — and must face what remains of the act, the home he thought he had outgrown, and the cost of being heard alone.
